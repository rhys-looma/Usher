## Part 3: branding (LU-58)

- A theme is data from closed lists, in `packages/core/src/theme.ts`:
  - display name;
  - logo: a PNG captured from the site's own pages, at most 48 KB;
  - accent and second colour;
  - typeface: one of six;
  - radius: 0, 4, 8, 12 or 16;
  - result fields: the extra figures and the thumbnail.
- Contrast is checked in code. Each colour moves to the nearest that passes (3:1 on the card, 4.5:1 for text on it), separately for light and dark.
- Derivation (`packages/registry/src/theme-derive.ts`) runs on join, and on start for the seeded demo site.
  - Usher's browser opens the home page and the first journey's page, on the site's own origin only. It collects names, logos, computed colours, fonts and corners, plus a masked screenshot.
  - Muse Spark chooses among them by index under a strict schema; code chooses when no model is available.
  - Off-origin logos are never candidates.
  - The result is a proposal: nothing is used until the owner accepts it.
- The owner's page is `/dashboard/theme`: the proposal, "Refresh from my site", an editor, and a live preview of the decision, result and ticket cards. The preview is the real card in sandboxed frames, light and dark.
- Cards: a band with the site's name, logo and a fixed "via Usher" mark; the site's accent, typeface and corners. The theme is in `structuredContent` only, never in the model's text. The plain-text result names the site.
- Brightwater's demo header gained a small wave mark of its own, so there is a real logo to find.
- Brightwater, read locally by code: 0.3 s. Result: name "Brightwater Insurance", accent and band #0b6e6e, its wave mark, system typeface, 4 px corners.
- Tests:
  - core: validation, contrast and lists;
  - registry: code and model derivation, the off-origin logo, the owner's page, the join trigger;
  - mcp: a theme cannot change what a card does, with hostile themes included; and the site app's results carry the theme and name the site.
